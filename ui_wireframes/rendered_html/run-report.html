Energy balance

TermRunBaselineΔNote
Read the residual, not the total. The ballistic phase is conservative by construction, so a non-zero residual measures integrator drift and nothing else. It is a check on the timestep, not a physical loss. Real dissipation — contact impulse, damper work, motor work — only appears once the Rapier core is attached, and those rows stay blank until then.

Published accuracy bounds

Ballistic phase

—

Position error against the closed-form projectile solution over this run's airtime. The trapezoidal velocity update is exact under constant gravity, so this phase is limited by floating-point precision — not by the timestep. The bound that matters is set by the contact and constraint phases, which arrive with the core.

Energy drift

—

Residual as a fraction of kinetic energy at the lip. The convergence sweep on the Validation page shows how this scales with Δt.
